# Power under the registered rules

The program reads training groups, held-out counts, and committed fits and amounts.
Training groups are resampled with replacement within each game at the held-out counts
and treated as independent. Open-loop scores use out-of-fold fits.
Closed-loop trial scores use laws and amounts fitted on those same training groups;
paired ensembles are drawn without replacement from a finite pool.
Coverage is of the trial expectation. Effects are shifts of location that keep
the full-effect variation; a real smaller effect would not keep that variation.
The effect rates are a guide, not the power of the registered test.
Each figure concerns one comparison with `alone`; nothing concerns the prompted arm.
The paste records one run and is not regenerated by a test.
