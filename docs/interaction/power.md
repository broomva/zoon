# Power under the registered rules

These are rates of the registered comparison rules, computed on training groups
before registration. This calculation reads training groups and held-out counts only.
Training groups stand in for held-out groups; the laws are treated as fixed.
The closed-loop figures are in-sample for the laws; groups are treated as independent.
Effects are shifts of location. Each figure concerns one comparison, rather than
both comparisons of a hypothesis together. Nothing here concerns the prompted arm.
The pasted output is a record of one run and is not regenerated by a test.
