These fixtures are regenerated by `python -m zoon.pilot.prompts`. Both prompted
records describe the first calibration participant, using the first rotation's
training examples; every participant here is from the training loader. The history
fixture uses the fifth trial, and the empty fixture uses the first.

Decision requests are JSON data with a state, question, and declared answers and
descriptions. Second-family requests are the exact text bytes, with no trailing
newline. Recognition is second-family only, as the pre-registration specifies.
The recognition request includes the full rewrite; judge states omit its final
question. Context labels use a letter, a period, and a space. Person labels are
followed by a newline; trials and the other prompt parts have one blank line
between them. The study introduction retains the pre-registration's line breaks.

Task texts and outcomes: Maier, Cheung and Lieder,
https://osf.io/4up5z/, CC BY 4.0
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Whitespace is joined during
mechanical extraction from https://osf.io/download/sgf5y/.
Judge rewrites carry their own source attribution in `pilot/rewrites/`.
